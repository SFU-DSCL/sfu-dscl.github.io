Overview

What this capability means

Supply chain data is only useful when people can understand it and act on it. DSCL helps partners transform complex data into clear visual tools that support communication, decision-making, and strategic planning.

What DSCL Provides

Applied support for partners

  • Interactive dashboards
  • Geospatial supply chain maps
  • Supply chain network visualizations
  • Performance indicator design
  • Risk and disruption dashboards
  • Timeline and event-sequence visualization
  • Scenario-comparison tools
  • Executive briefing graphics
  • Visual storytelling for reports and presentations

Why It Matters

Supply chain relevance

Supply chains involve many actors, locations, time points, and dependencies. A spreadsheet or static report may not show how problems move through a system. Visualization helps partners understand not only what is happening, but where, when, why, and with what consequence. It also creates a shared language for government, industry, and academic partners.

Partner Opportunity

Example project directions

  • Building a dashboard for supply chain performance indicators
  • Mapping freight flows, bottlenecks, or infrastructure dependencies
  • Visualizing disruption scenarios and downstream impacts
  • Creating partner-facing visual reports for collaborative projects
  • Designing executive graphics for funding, policy, or investment decisions

Outcome

What partners gain

A successful visualization project turns data into insight, insight into alignment, and alignment into action.
